Forensic Standards & Frequently Asked Questions

Understanding the science behind deepfake detection, cryptographic PDF verification certificates, and privacy principles.

What this actually is: a heuristic analysis tool, not a trained AI-detection model. Modern generators are good enough that no test — including commercial ones — is reliable on its own. Treat results as one input among several (source, context, reverse image search), never as proof.

Why ELA & Fourier Analysis?

While AI image generators produce photorealistic faces, they operate by denoising random Gaussian fields. This leaves micro-lattice artifacts in the frequency domain (Fourier FFT) and abnormal error quantization levels (ELA) that are flagged by mathematical signal inspection.

100% Client-Side Privacy

Scanline runs all image decoding, HTML5 canvas ELA generation, and PDF report assembly locally within your web browser memory. Zero user uploaded images leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

What generative AI models can Scanline analyze?
Scanline evaluates Midjourney v4-v6, FLUX.1 (Dev/Schnell), Stable Diffusion 1.5 / XL / 3, DALL-E 3, Adobe Firefly, StyleGAN2/3 portraits, and DeepFaceLab face swap modifications.
How is the Forensic PDF Report generated?
The PDF report is generated dynamically in client-side JavaScript using jsPDF. It includes a unique Report UUID, timestamp, cryptographic SHA-256 hash token, score breakdown table, and embedded Error Level Analysis (ELA) heatmap thumbnails.
Where is my audit history stored?
All audit logs are stored securely inside your browser's localStorage. You can search, filter, export as JSON, or clear history at any time from the Audit Logs page.
